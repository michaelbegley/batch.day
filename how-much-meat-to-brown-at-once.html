How much meat can I brown at once?

About 1½ lb at a time in a 12-inch pan. More than that crowds the pan and the meat steams. 5 lb takes 4 batches.

4 batches

About 24 minutes of searing in a 12-inch pan.

How it works

  • Pounds divided by 1½, rounded up, is the number of batches.
  • It plans about 6 minutes a batch.
  • Set each batch aside while you sear the next.
  • Don't crowd the pan, or the meat steams.

Questions
