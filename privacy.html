batch.recipes

Privacy

Last updated 13 November 2026

batch.recipes turns recipe screenshots into a batch-cooking plan. There are no accounts, so we never know who you are.

Screenshots

When you add screenshots, they are sent to our reader on Hugging Face. The reader passes them to an AI model through Hugging Face Inference Providers and sends back the recipe text. The reader doesn't save the screenshots or the text. To enforce read limits it keeps your IP address in memory for up to a day, and nothing else.

  • Screenshots are only sent when you add them. Nothing is sent in the background.
  • If the AI reader doesn't answer or is over its limit, your screenshots are read in your browser instead. If you're offline, they aren't sent at all.
  • Don't add screenshots that show anything private.

What stays on your device

  • Recipe text you paste is read in your browser and isn't uploaded.
  • Recipes are saved in your browser's storage under My batches. Clearing your browser data removes them. We can't see or recover them.
  • When you use Copy link or Send, the recipe goes inside the link itself. The part of a link after the # isn't sent to our servers.

Services the site uses

  • Vercel hosts the site. Like any web host, it handles basic request information such as your IP address so the page can load.
  • Hugging Face runs the AI reader described above and passes screenshots to the AI model through its Inference Providers.
  • jsDelivr serves the in-browser reader, which is only loaded if the AI reader can't be used.
  • Vercel Web Analytics counts visits and a few anonymous actions, like reading a recipe or opening cook mode. It uses no cookies and stores no personal information. We use it to see whether the site is worth keeping.

Advertising

batch.recipes may show ads from Google AdSense to keep the site free.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your earlier visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's advertising cookies let Google and its partners serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and other sites.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies at aboutads.info.
  • Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are used. You can change your choice any time with the privacy button on the page.

California and other US states

Showing personalized ads can count as "selling" or "sharing" personal information under laws in California and some other US states. batch.recipes doesn't sell anything else about you.

  • Use the Do not sell or share my personal information option on the site to opt out. Ads will still show, but they won't be personalized.
  • If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, Google treats it as an opt-out.
  • You can also ask about your information through the contact below.

Learn more in how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Children

batch.recipes isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their information.
