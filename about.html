batch.recipes

About

batch.recipes is a free tool for people who cook once and eat all week.

Add a screenshot of any recipe, from TikTok, Reels or a blog, and it plans your batch day:

  • how much to buy for the number of meals you want
  • which pots and pans fit
  • what to cook first
  • what goes in the freezer, in the fridge and fresh at serving

The scaling follows real batch-cooking rules, not just multiplication. Salt, spices and oil are eased back in big batches. Rice and stews are sized to fit the pot. Baking gets a warning, because it doesn't scale well past double.

It works on your phone with no account. Your recipes stay on your device.

Made by Michael Begley.
