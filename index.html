    How it scales

    • Meat, vegetables, grains and broth scale straight up.
    • Salt, spices and oil are eased back past double. Taste at the end and add more.
    • Lime, herbs and other fresh finishers are kept for serving.
    • Stews fill a pot three-quarters at most, dry rice a third. Meat is seared in batches.
    • Baking isn't eased back, and gets a warning past double.
