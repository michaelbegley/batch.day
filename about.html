About batch.recipes

batch.recipes is a free website that turns any recipe into a batch-cooking plan. Add a screenshot or the text of a recipe, pick how many meals you want, and it gives you a grocery list, a cook order and a fridge and freezer plan.

It's for people who cook once and eat all week: meal preppers, budget cooks and anyone who finds recipes on TikTok, Instagram Reels or food blogs.

What you get

  • A scaled recipe for the number of meals you pick, in US cups, ounces and pounds.
  • A grocery list in store order: produce, meat, dairy, canned goods, dry goods, spices. Tick items as you shop, or send the list to Reminders, Notes, Google Keep or any list app.
  • A cook order, one step at a time, with the screen kept on.
  • A storage plan: what goes in the freezer, what goes in the fridge and what to add fresh when you eat.
  • Freezer labels to print on Avery 5160 sheets or save as an image.

How the scaling works

It follows batch-cooking rules, not plain multiplication. Meat, vegetables, grains and broth scale straight up. Salt, spices and oil are eased back past double, so a big batch isn't over-seasoned. Fresh finishers like lime and herbs are kept for serving. Pots are sized so stews fill no more than three-quarters and dry rice no more than a third. Baking isn't eased back and gets a warning past double, because it doesn't scale well.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. There's no account and no sign-up. Store links and ads keep it free.

Does it work with TikTok and Instagram recipes?

Yes. Screenshot the recipe and add the screenshots. An AI model reads the ingredients and steps. If it can't, your phone reads them instead.

Where are my recipes saved?

On your phone or computer, not on a server. You can back them up to a file and restore them on a new phone. Screenshots are read and not kept. More in Privacy.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes, in the browser. You can add it to your Home Screen to use it like an app.
