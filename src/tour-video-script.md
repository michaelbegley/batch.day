# batch.recipes: how it works (30 seconds)

Same five beats as the in-app tour. Vertical 9:16, phone screen recording, light mode. Use the sample recipe (Brothy chicken and rice) so the video matches what the tour shows.

Voiceover: 68 words. Read it plain, no hype.

| # | Time | Voiceover | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00 to 0:06 | Found a recipe on TikTok or Reels? Screenshot it, then add the screenshots here. | Home page. Thumb taps **Choose screenshots**, picks 3 screenshots. The reading screen runs for a second, then the recipe page opens. Caption: Add screenshots |
| 2 | 0:06 to 0:11 | Set how many meals you want. It scales the whole recipe. | Tap **+** on Meals, 14 up to 16. The amounts in the list change. Caption: Set how many meals |
| 3 | 0:11 to 0:19 | That's your grocery list, in store order. Tick as you shop, or send it to your list app. | Shop tab. Scroll the list once, tick two items, the count by the heading goes up. Tap **Add to list**. Caption: Grocery list |
| 4 | 0:19 to 0:25 | Then cook one step at a time. The screen stays on. | Tap **Start cooking**. Swipe through two steps in cook mode. Caption: Cook step by step |
| 5 | 0:25 to 0:30 | Save it or share it when you're done. It's free at batch dot recipes. | Close cook mode, tap **Share**, the sheet opens. Cut to the pot logo and batch.recipes. Caption: batch.recipes |

## Notes
- Captions are the short on-screen words, burned in. They match the tour bubbles.
- Beat 1 needs real screenshots of a recipe in the camera roll. The tour uses the sample here.
- "The screen stays on" is true on phones that support it (current iPhone and Android browsers).
- "Add to list" shows on phones with a share sheet. Record on a phone, not a desktop.
- No music needed. If there is some, keep it under the voice.
